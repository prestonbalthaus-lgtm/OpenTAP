## 3. Invariants and rules in force

### 3.1 The six crate conventions (lib.rs:11-42)

1. Every structure is `#[repr(C)]`, no padding, derives `FromBytes, IntoBytes, Immutable, KnownLayout`; page-sized ones are `align(4096)`; record slices are views too; zero-copy views need an aligned buffer (`Block4K`/`Page16K`/arena), never a `Vec<u8>`; the `read`/`copy_out` paths copy and work anywhere.
2. Little-endian only (`compile_error!` on big-endian).
3. Every byte is under a CRC-32C: fixed records carry it in their last four bytes over everything before; headerless pages are covered by the CRC in the parent reference; reserved fields are written zero and covered.
4. `ver = major << 8 | minor`. A reader accepts any minor of its own major; a later minor may only give meaning to reserved bytes, which readers ignore; a higher major is a hard stop (F7). Enumerated fields must hold defined values. On the wire there are no reserved bytes and unknown kinds/codecs/flags are refused.
5. Sizes, offsets, discriminants and decoders are `const`-asserted in each module.
6. `parse` returns a verified view, `read` a verified copy; the raw zerocopy conversions verify nothing; **the seal/builder paths validate what `parse` would reject, so a builder cannot write an image its reader refuses.**

### 3.2 Rulings an implementer of (B) must keep

- **F5** (lib.rs:104-107): CP numbers are `u64` in headers and `u32` as `birth_cp`; every header CP is checked against `MAX_CP_SEQ`; CP 0 is mkfs, so a birth is `>= 1`.
- **F7** (lib.rs:111-117): a slot from a newer major is a hard error, never a fallback; commit `N` writes `A(N mod 2)` then `B(N mod 2)`.
- **F12** (lib.rs:156-166): `cp_log_head` names a directory of delta regions since the last fold, in CP order, plus the pages the writing CP wrote; "Every region is named exactly (block, length, CRC), as E14 requires."
- **F13** (lib.rs:167-171): a snapshot pins a root through a `SnapRootPage`; snapshots are taken at fold CPs.
- **F14** (lib.rs:172-179): `map_roots` is one `MapRoot` per volume index up to `vol_count`; `snap_table` is the `SnapRecord`s in ascending id order; both padded to whole pages and sealed by the superblock's root reference.
- **F16** (lib.rs:202-211): the catalogue: 512-byte records, names 1-255 bytes UTF-8 without control chars or `/`, unique among volumes and among a volume's snapshots, canonical order, per-record CRC plus region root; "A commit that changed no name carries the previous region forward unchanged."
- **F17** (lib.rs:212-220): every commit lists the data stripes it wrote; the boot verifies each by summary (CRC and identity, nonce included) before trusting the commit.
- **F18** (lib.rs:221-228): `VOL_OFFLINE`; `page_entries` may be non-zero in any CP; `SegState::Relocated` and `reloc_index` stay reserved: a drained segment is freed, not relocated (E37).
- **E14** (docs/07:282-310): recovery reads exact roots; a boot trusts only what a root names and never looks at a stripe or page no committed CP named; "A CP sequence number may be re-issued after a rewind."
- **E22** (docs/07:618-680): the delta-region + directory + fold model for the map; fold policy belongs to the engine (`FoldPolicy`); "a fold CP writes no delta region of its own"; replay applies a delta iff its CP is above the fold CP; delta regions of a fold are freed under the deferred-free rule of E24.
- **E35** (docs/07:1104-1150): what 2.3 added and the closing rule "Any later field takes bytes from `reserved` and bumps the minor again"; the engine boots 2.3 pools only (`BootError::OldLayout`), recorded as the exception to the "any minor" rule; the format crate still decodes the older vector sets.
- **docs/09 section 5** (97-111), the format-change procedure: (1) bump `FORMAT_MINOR`/`STREAM_MINOR`; (2) write the new vectors into a new `tests/vectors/v2.<minor>/` with `cargo test -p otap-format -- --ignored write_golden_vectors --nocapture`, pin the printed hash, keep the previous directory; (3) record an F-ruling in the crate root (layout) or an S-rule in docs/07 (wire) and the event in the log; (4) a reader accepts any minor of its own major and ignores reserved bytes on disk. "The one exception, regenerating the current vector directory in place, was used once (2026-10-13, before any release) and is closed."
- **Check order** is magic, then version, then CRC, then fields (`adversarial_tests.rs:1331 checks_run_in_the_order_magic_version_crc_fields`); every `verify` in the crate follows it.
- **Region images**: zero-padded to whole 4 KiB pages, at least one page (`snap_table_image` 1251, `catalog_image` 264, `cp_stripes_image` 1401, `map_roots_image` 554, `CpLogDir::encode`). `RootRef::for_region` (types.rs:504) and `MetaStream::place_region` (meta.rs:241-243) refuse anything else.
- **The empty-slot sentinel**: `parse_snap_table`, `parse_catalog` and `parse_cp_stripes` stop at the first all-zero record and demand zeros after it. A record type whose valid encoding can be all zero cannot live in such a region.
- **Empty regions**: `snap_table_image(&[])` and `catalog_image(&[])` give one zero page (parse to an empty list), but the engine writes `RootRef::NONE` instead when the list is empty (engine.rs:5175-5177, 5224-5228); the boot treats `is_none()` as "no records" (boot.rs:473, 493).
- **Superblock rebuilt from zero every commit** (superblock.rs:36-41, 519-521): nothing read from disk is carried into a new page except through explicit engine state (`self.catalog_ref`, roots).
- **Reserved bytes are ignored by readers** everywhere except one place: `parse_cp_stripes` refuses a non-zero `CpStripe::rsvd` (records.rs:1424-1426). `CatalogRec.flags/rsvd0/rsvd1`, `SnapRecord.rsvd`, `SegEntry.rsvd`, `MapRoot.flags/rsvd0/rsvd1`, `CpLogPageHdr.rsvd`, `CpLogDirHdr.rsvd`, `SnapRootPage.rsvd0/reserved`, `Superblock.reserved`, `StripeHdr.flags`, descriptor `rsvd` bits are all ignored (see section 7).

## 4. How things happen, in order

### 4.1 A commit builds the snapshot table and the catalogue today (engine.rs, `prepare`)

1. The surviving snapshots (`remaining`) are updated in place: rewritten trees get a new `SnapRootPage` placed with `stream.place_region(page.as_bytes())` and `record.map_root_pba = page_ref.pba; record.seal()` (engine.rs:5088-5098); pending pin changes become `record.flags` + `seal()` (5121-5128).
2. New snapshots (only at a fold, `if fold`) get `SnapRootPage::new(agg_id, snap_id, job.cp_seq, root, RootRef::NONE)`, a placed page, and a `SnapRecord` literal with `map_root_pba = page_ref.pba`, `deadlist_pba: 0`, `birth_cp: cp32`, `flags: 0`, sealed (5133-5164). `snap_id = self.next_snap_id + i`.
3. `after = remaining ++ new`; `records` collected and `sort_by_key(snap_id)`; **`snap_table = NONE if empty else place_region(&snap_table_image(&records)?)`** -- rewritten whole every CP, changed or not (5166-5179).
4. The catalogue is rebuilt from scratch: one `CatalogRec::volume(vol, name, created)` per present, non-deleted, named volume, keeping `created_wall_ns` from the existing record (so a rename keeps the original time) (5184-5198); for each snapshot in `after`, the existing record verbatim, else a fresh `CatalogRec::snapshot(vol, uuid, name, wall)` for a snapshot requested with a name (5199-5220); `sort_by_key(CatalogRec::sort_key)` (5221).
5. **Carry-forward**: `if catalog == self.catalog && !self.catalog_ref.is_none() { self.catalog_ref } else if empty { free old; NONE } else { free old; place_region(&catalog_image(&catalog)?) }` (5222-5234). The old region goes to `freed_other` (deferred free, E24).
6. Then `cp_stripes` (5237-5244), `map_roots = place_region(&map_roots_image(&prepared.roots_table)?)` (5249), the directory `dir = prepared.dir.clone(); dir.pages.extend(extra_pages); cp_log_head = place_region(&dir.encode(agg_id)?)` (5250-5252), and the segment table reserved last (5253-5254).
7. `table_regions = [map_roots, cp_log_head, snap_table, cp_stripes, seg_table]` minus `NONE` (5398-5401) -- note the catalogue is **not** in `table_regions` (it is accounted separately as carried metadata); `PreparedParts` carries `snap_table, catalog, catalog_ref, cp_stripes` (5424-5447).
8. `finalize` writes the roots into a fresh page: `sb.seg_table, sb.cp_log_head, sb.map_roots, sb.snap_table = prep.snap_table, sb.catalog = prep.catalog_ref, sb.cp_stripes` (5608-5613); `vol_count = max carried vol + 1` (5619-5620).
9. After the commit lands: `self.catalog = catalog; self.catalog_ref = catalog_ref` (5737-5738); snapshot state updated (5739-...).

`MetaStream::place_region(bytes)` (meta.rs:241-250) = `reserve(pages)` + `place_at(pba, bytes)` (220-238), which copies into the chunk and returns `RootRef::for_region(pba, bytes)` -- the region CRC is over the padded image.

### 4.2 The boot reads them (boot.rs `walk_cp`, 385-533)

1. `map_roots`: `read_root` + `parse_map_roots(&bytes, sb.vol_count)`; every present root's index via `index_root_ref` + `root.verify_index`; every non-none page entry becomes a region via `page_root_ref` (395-422).
2. `cp_log_head`: `CpLogDir::decode(&bytes, agg_id)`; **`dir.cp_seq != sb.cp_seq` tears the candidate** (424-444); every delta region and every listed page is read and verified (445-456).
3. `cp_stripes`: `parse_cp_stripes` (458-465); verified later per stripe (629-670).
4. `catalog`: an I/O error fails the candidate; a `read_root`/`parse_catalog` failure is **tolerated**: `(Vec::new(), RootRef::NONE, Some(reason))` and the boot goes on (466-487; review defect W4, `review_step5_sim_tests.rs:529-572`).
5. `snap_table`: `parse_snap_table` failure is **`TornReason::Snapshot` and tears the candidate** (493-498); then each record's root page via `load_snapshot` (538-575): `SnapRootPage::read`, identity (`agg_id`, `snap_id`, `birth_cp`, `root.vol`) must match the record, index verified; damage to a snapshot the *current* CP took tears the candidate, older damage is reported (499-517).
6. `read_root` (774-796): `NONE` gives empty; `pba != 0`, `end <= capacity`, `bytes <= 4 GiB` else `TornReason::RootOutOfRange{field}`; read; `root.verify(&buf)`.
7. After the walk the engine reconciles the catalogue: a record is kept only if it names a present volume or a snapshot in the table (same uuid and vol); others are dropped with an `Event::Internal` (engine.rs:1922-1955); `catalog_damage` becomes `OperatorWarning::CatalogDamaged` (1925-1931).
8. The boot's format floor: `(newest.sb.ver & 0xff) < 3` gives `BootError::OldLayout` (boot.rs:268-271; message "this release reads 2.3 and later", 87-92; doc 55-58).

### 4.3 The golden-vector procedure (format_tests.rs 2010-2834, BUILDING.md 148-168)

1. `VectorSet { dir, manifest_blake3, format_version, header, names }` (2018-2024); `VECTOR_SETS: [VectorSet; 4]` oldest first (2088-2117): v2.0 (`73b1f4d3...3af6`, 12 names), v2.1 (`e85ac0be...ac61`, 14), v2.2 (`3f9a9651...168d`, 14), v2.3 (`6ba96829...425b`, 16). `current_set()` is the **last** entry (2120-2122).
2. `golden_images()` (2455-2497) builds every image with the current builders and fixed constants (`GOLDEN_NONCE 0x0123_4567_89AB_CDEF`, `uuid(seed)`, literal wall-clock numbers); the manifest order is that list's order: `seg_entries, snap_records, cp_log_page, superblock_slot, superblock_slot_minor1, intent_slot, stripe_summary, map_page, page_index_page, map_root, stream_frame, hole_records, cp_log_dir, snap_root_page, catalog, cp_stripes`.
3. `manifest_text` (2499-2513): first line `otap-format golden vectors: format {MAJOR}.{MINOR} stream {MAJOR}.{MINOR}`, then one line per image `"<blake3 hex>  <size>  <name>"` (two spaces).

### 4.4 `CpLogDir` encode/decode (records.rs:1010-1107) -- the model for a "head"

Encode: bounds; totals into every page header; `page.push(root)?` and on `Ok(None)` seal, emit, open page `page_seq + 1`, push again; seal and emit the last page (always at least one). Decode: length check; per page `read` (verify), `agg_id`, `page_seq == i`, agreement with page 0; totals; split. The engine carries the delta list across CPs itself: `otap-map` keeps `self.delta_roots`, and `PreparedCp.dir.deltas = if fold { this CP's } else { self.delta_roots ++ this CP's }` with `fold_cp_seq: fold_cp_after` and `freed_deltas = self.delta_roots.clone()` on a fold (otap-map/src/lib.rs:1343-1358, `PreparedCp` 509-530).

## 5. Hook points for Step 6 (B)

### 5.1 Conventions a new page/record type must follow (the checklist, with the model for each)

1. `#[derive(FromBytes, IntoBytes, Immutable, KnownLayout, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Hash, Debug)]` (+ `Default` for records that have an all-zero meaning), `#[repr(C)]` for records, `#[repr(C, align(4096))]` for pages (records.rs:593-594, 652-653, 1121-1122). No padding: every field width adds up and the const asserts prove it.
2. A page header is 64 bytes with **magic `u32 @0`, `ver u16 @4`**, a `u16` count @6, then identity **`cp_seq u64`, `page_seq`, `agg_id Uuid`, `cp_nonce u64`**, reserved zero bytes, and **`crc32c u32 @60` over `0..60`** (`CpLogPageHdr`, records.rs:595-614). Magic and `ver` never move (constants.rs:175). `verify` = `check_magic`, `check_major(ver, FORMAT_MAJOR)`, `check_crc("<name>")`, then bounds (`count <= capacity`, `cp_seq <= MAX_CP_SEQ`) -- never a check on the minor, never on reserved bytes.
3. A sealed whole page (no record array) puts the CRC in the last four bytes over `0..4092`, keeps a `reserved [u8; N]` field so the size is exactly 4096, and validates every root it carries as "exactly `NONE` or `pba != 0`" (`SnapRootPage`, records.rs:1123-1215).
4. Records carry their own trailing CRC over everything before it (`SnapRecord` over `0..60`, `CatalogRec` over `0..508`); `compute_crc()` + `seal()` + `validate()` + `verify()` are the four methods, in that naming (records.rs:403-477, catalog.rs:143-223).
5. **Builders refuse what parsers refuse**: `new`/`open`/`volume`/`snapshot`/`*_image`/`encode` run `validate()` (or the region checks) before `seal()` and return `Result<_, FormatError>` (records.rs:170-191, 1149-1167; catalog.rs:108-126; `catalog_tests.rs:122-126` says it in words).
6. Error naming: `bad_field(field, value)` with the **field name as written in the struct** and the value read, or the record's index when the complaint is about a list (`"sort_key", i`; `"name", i`; `"idx", i`; `"count", n`); `OutOfRange { field, value, max }` for bounds; `IdentityMismatch { field }` for a verified structure that is not the one asked for; `BadLength { expected, found }` with the length that *would* have been right (`bytes.len().div_ceil(BLOCK_SIZE) * BLOCK_SIZE`, records.rs:1261).
7. Enums are `#[repr(u8)]` with `const fn from_u8 -> Option<Self>` and `const fn as_u8`, discriminants and decoders const-asserted (records.rs:48-74, 725-757).
8. Const asserts at the bottom of the module: `size_of`, `align_of`, every `offset_of!`, and the arithmetic (`HDR + N * REC == PAGE`) (records.rs:795-812).
9. Region builders pad to whole pages (`div_ceil(BLOCK_SIZE).max(1) * BLOCK_SIZE`), parsers demand a multiple of 4096 and zeros after the data, and all region parsing is by **copy** (`copy_out`, `read`), never by aligned view, because the engine reads regions into `Vec<u8>` (records.rs:1241-1286, boot.rs:791).
10. Identity checks of a multi-page region live in the region decoder, not in the page `verify` (`CpLogDir::decode`, records.rs:1072-1089).
11. Every `pub` item documented (`deny(missing_docs)`); doc comments say which bytes the CRC covers and what each reserved field is.
12. Add the type to `lib.rs` re-exports and the layout table; add it to `format_tests.rs struct_sizes_are_frozen` / `struct_alignments_are_frozen` / an offsets test, to `adversarial_tests.rs every_parser_refuses_every_other_structure_by_magic` (1556), and give it a golden image.

## 7. Risks, pitfalls and traps

1. **The minor1 vector trap at byte 1760.** The current golden `superblock_slot_minor1.bin` writes `ver = FORMAT_VERSION + 1` (= `0x0204`!) and `[1,2,3,4]` into `reserved[..4]` (format_tests.rs:2201-2208), i.e. bytes 1760..1764 -- exactly where the first 2.4 field will live. A 2.4 reader will interpret those bytes as that field. If the field is a `RootRef`, the image reads as `RootRef { pba: 0x0403_0201, pages: 0, crc: 0 }`, `is_none()`, harmless, and the lane can assert it as it does for 2.2 (2660-2667). If the field is something `verify` constrains when `minor >= 4`, the v2.3 lane must expect a *failure* on that field as the 2.1 lane does (2638-2652). Decide before writing `decode_set`.
2. **`FORMAT_VERSION` is pinned in three files** (constants.rs:220, catalog_tests.rs:51, records_tests.rs:426) and `SUPERBLOCK_RESERVED` in two (constants.rs:50, catalog_tests.rs:50) plus the offsets tests; the build fails loudly on the first, the tests on the rest.
3. **`write_golden_vectors` refuses to run until the new `VectorSet` is appended** (2519-2522) and `read_set` fails until its hash is pinned, so the sequence is: add the set with a placeholder hash, run with `--ignored --nocapture`, pin, run the lane. The hash is BLAKE3 of the manifest **file bytes** (newline-terminated lines).
4. **Builders must be deterministic**: the lane byte-compares the newest set with `golden_images()` on every run (2591-2596). No wall clock, no randomness, no `HashMap` iteration order in a builder.
5. **Zero-page sentinel vs. deletes**: a tombstone record must have a non-zero discriminant in its first bytes, or `parse_*` will take it for the end of the region. `CpStripe` survives because `pseg != 0` is required; `CatalogRec` because `kind != 0`; `SnapRecord` because `snap_id != 0`.
6. **Two styles of unused slots**: `CpLogPage::verify` ignores records beyond `count` (records_tests.rs:344-351 asserts a flip there is "not part of the page's meaning"), while `CpLogDirPage::verify` demands `NONE` beyond `count`. Pick one per page type and test it either way.
7. **Page headers do not check identity**: `CpLogPageHdr::verify` ignores `agg_id`/`cp_nonce`/`page_seq`; only `CpLogDir::decode` checks `agg_id` and `page_seq`. A delta-region decoder must do the same or a stale page from another aggregate/CP at a reused location would pass (E14 makes that unlikely but the directory still checks).
8. **`CpLogPage::new` takes `page_seq: u64`, `CpLogDirPage::new` takes `u32`**; the header widths differ (`u64 @16` vs `u32 @48`). Copying one header into a new type and the other's constructor signature is an easy slip.
9. **The per-record CRC is belt and braces**: the region `RootRef` covers every byte anyway; `CpLogRec` carries one, `CpStripe` and `RootRef` entries do not. The module doc "a torn page is cut at the first record that fails" (records.rs:28) is not what the code does: `CpLogPage::verify` returns the first error (705-708) and the boot tears the candidate on it (boot.rs:616-618). Doc/code disagreement.
10. **`parse_cp_stripes` refuses non-zero `rsvd`** (records.rs:1424-1426) although convention 4 says reserved bytes are ignored; `catalog_tests.rs:194-199` tests the opposite rule for `CatalogRec`. A 2.4 writer may not give meaning to `CpStripe.rsvd`. Worth settling in the same minor bump.
11. **Flag bytes are not reserved bytes**: `SnapRecord::flags` upper bits are *refused* (`flags & !PIN_MASK`), `CatalogRec::flags` is *ignored*, `MapRoot::flags` is *ignored*, `StripeHdr::flags` is *ignored*. Only `SnapRecord.rsvd` (@59) and the explicit `rsvd*` fields are free.
12. **Boot damage classes differ per region** and are tested: a catalogue that fails to parse is dropped and reported (boot.rs:466-487; W4); a snapshot table that fails to parse **tears the candidate** (497-498); a damaged older snapshot root page is reported, a damaged one of the current CP tears (499-517). A region that is *carried forward unchanged* is named by every candidate of the ring, so one damaged byte in it tears all of them (`review_step5_sim_tests.rs:529-532`). Table deltas carried across many CPs put the snapshot table into exactly that position; the design must say which class a damaged delta falls in (section 8, Q4).
13. **A directory is rewritten every CP by design** (`CpLogDir::encode` always emits at least one page; boot requires `dir.cp_seq == sb.cp_seq`). A table head with the same identity rule costs one page per CP whatever happens; a headerless, carried-forward head (the catalogue precedent) costs nothing on a quiet CP but has no identity of its own.
14. **The snapshot table is rewritten whole on every CP today** (engine.rs:5173-5179) with no carry-forward compare, unlike the catalogue; at 500 snapshots that is 8 pages per CP. The catalogue is already O(1) per CP in steady state (it changes only on a name operation). A zero-format-change improvement exists (compare and carry forward the `snap_table` ref, as 5222 does for the catalogue); it does not meet "incremental" when snapshots change every CP (relocation rewrites, pins) but it is the floor to measure the format change against.
15. **`lseg == pseg` is assumed by the boot** (boot.rs:634 `let (lseg, idx) = (entry.pseg, entry.idx)`) because `SegState::Relocated` is reserved (F18). The attestation pass must not derive `lseg` from the `StripeHdr` for a stripe it located by `pseg` without checking that equality holds in the segment table (`SegEntry.phys`).
16. **Map addresses are segment-relative block offsets including the summary pages**: `MapEntry::off()` is the 4 KiB block within the segment; the stripe is `off / (stripe_bytes / 4096)` and the data block index inside it is `(off % blocks_per_stripe) - summary_pages` (`SummaryLayout::block_offset(i) = summary_bytes + i*4096`, stripe.rs:516-519; `DiskLayout::stripe_pba(pseg, idx)` at layout.rs:189 and `stripe_of_pba` at 319). I did not read `layout.rs`; confirm the arithmetic there before writing the per-block CRC check.
17. **Hole CRCs differ by structure**: a hole's descriptor carries `ZERO_BLOCK_CRC32C` and its data slot is present and zero (F9), while the map entry (and `CpLogRec`) carry CRC 0 for holes and unmapped blocks. An attestation comparing `MapEntry.crc32c` with `StripeDescriptor.crc32c` must special-case holes.
18. **`parse_map_roots` needs `vol_count`**; `vol_count` is "max carried volume index + 1" (engine.rs:5619), not the number of present volumes; `parse_catalog` only bounds `vol <= MAX_VOL`, so a catalogue may name a volume index at or above `vol_count` and the engine drops it at boot (1932-1955).
19. **`Superblock::verify` applies the geometry rule only when `minor >= 2`** (623-626) and the engine refuses `geometry() == None` pools; a 2.4 field gated on `minor >= 4` must likewise tolerate a 2.3 page in the format crate even though the engine will refuse it.
20. **`place_region` wants a non-empty multiple of 4096** (meta.rs:242-243); a delta image of zero ops must either not be placed (write `NONE`) or be one zero page -- and a zero page parses as "no ops" only if the parser treats the all-zero sentinel as the end.
21. **Golden image of a `ver`-carrying structure changes hash on every minor** (section 4.3); do not be surprised that `cp_log_page.bin` etc. differ across sets while `snap_records.bin` does not.
22. **`unreachable_pub` and `missing_docs`** are warnings/denials: a `pub(crate)` helper must stay `pub(crate)`; every new `pub` item needs a sentence.
23. Toolchain idioms already in use and required: `slice::as_chunks::<N>()` (records.rs:1066, stripe.rs:698), `usize::is_multiple_of` (types.rs:504), `Option::is_none_or` (boot.rs:549) -- Rust 1.89 per the workspace pin.
24. The memory note says the owner's PC froze under parallel builds: keep core 0 free and run the 100k gate alone.

Doc/code disagreements found (collected): lib.rs:68 heading "F1 to F15" (F16-F18 exist); records.rs:15-20 "Names ... are not on disk here" (superseded by F16; lib.rs:141-143 is updated, the module doc is not); constants.rs:131 calls `SnapRecord` a "snapshot catalogue record" (collides with `CatalogRec`); records.rs:28 "cut at the first record that fails" vs the error-returning `verify`; format_tests.rs:10 and 2010 still say "v2.0 and v2.1 / v1.0" lanes (four sets exist); convention 4 vs `parse_cp_stripes`'s `rsvd` check. E22's "14 files, manifest e85ac0be...13ac61" and E35's offsets (1,728 / 1,744 / 2,332) agree with the code.

## 8. Open questions for the lead (with the proposal)

### 8.1 The smallest format addition that gives per-CP table deltas and a head (a proposal, not a decision)

**F19 (draft).** Three additions, all modelled on existing types:

(a) **One new superblock root, `table_deltas: RootRef` at byte 1760** (`reserved` becomes 2316). The bases stay where they are: `snap_table` @448 and `catalog` @1728 keep their meaning *as of the last table fold*; a 2.4 commit that folds writes fresh bases and a `NONE` head. (Placing the root at 1760 keeps the v2.3 minor1 image decodable, as 2.2 to 2.3 did.)

(b) **A "table directory" region** named by `table_deltas`: a clone of `CpLogDirHdr`/`CpLogDirPage`/`CpLogDir` with magic `OTTH` (or `OTTD`), fields `magic, ver, count, cp_seq, fold_cp_seq, agg_id, delta_entries, rsvd u32 (page_entries dropped), page_seq, rsvd[8], crc32c`, and the same `encode`/`decode` discipline (totals in every page, `agg_id`, `page_seq == i`). `fold_cp_seq` = the CP that wrote the current bases; `deltas` = the delta regions of CPs `fold_cp_seq+1 ..= cp_seq` in CP order, any table. Written every CP like `cp_log_head` (one page while there are at most 252 deltas), or carried forward unchanged when no delta was added (decide; pitfall 13).

(c) **One delta page type** with a `CpLogPageHdr`-shaped 64-byte header, a new magic, `table: u8` spent from `rsvd` (`1 = snapshot table, 2 = catalogue`), and two concrete page structs sharing the header:
   - `SnapDeltaPage { hdr, ops: [SnapOp; 56] }` with `SnapOp { op: u8 @0 (1 Upsert, 2 Delete), rsvd: [u8;7] @1, record: SnapRecord @8 }` = 72 B; `64 + 56*72 == 4096` exactly. Upsert requires `record.verify()`; Delete requires `record.snap_id != 0` and every other byte of the record zero (no CRC: the region root covers it). Ops in strictly ascending `snap_id`, each id at most once per delta.
   - `CatalogDeltaPage { hdr, ops: [CatalogOp; 7], pad: [u8; 392] }` with `CatalogOp { op u8, rsvd [u8;7], record: CatalogRec }` = 520 B; `64 + 7*520 + 392 == 4096`. Upsert requires `record.verify()`; Delete requires `kind`, `vol`, `snap_uuid` set as the key and the rest zero. Ops in strictly ascending `sort_key`, each key once.
   - Region builders/parsers `snap_delta_image(agg_id, cp_seq, cp_nonce, &[SnapOp])` / `parse_snap_delta(bytes, &agg_id) -> (cp_seq, Vec<SnapOp>)` and the catalogue pair, decoding like `CpLogDir::decode` (every page agrees, `page_seq == i`).
   - Pure fold functions `apply_snap_delta(&mut Vec<SnapRecord>, &[SnapOp]) -> Result` / `apply_catalog_delta(..)` that insert/replace/remove by key and re-run the base invariants (`snap_table_image`'s ascending-id rule; `check_catalog`'s order and name uniqueness) so the folded table is exactly what `snap_table_image`/`catalog_image` would accept.

Per-CP cost with this: 0 pages when no table changed (if the head is carried forward) or 1 page (if rewritten); 1 delta page + the head when up to 56 snapshot ops or 7 names changed. A fold rewrites the bases as today and frees the deltas under E24 (the `freed_deltas` pattern). Vectors: `v2.4/` = the 16 images (superblock with the new root) + `table_dir.bin`, `snap_delta.bin`, `catalog_delta.bin`.

Alternatives the lead may prefer, each smaller in one dimension:
- **Option 0 (no format change)**: carry the `snap_table` ref forward unchanged when the record list is equal (mirror engine.rs:5222 for snapshots). O(1) on quiet CPs; still a whole rewrite on any snapshot change. The floor to compare against.
- **Option B (CoW table pages, no deltas, no fold)**: make `snap_table`/`catalog` name a `CpLogDirPage`-shaped index of page `RootRef`s, each page a headerless run of 64 `SnapRecord`s / 8 `CatalogRec`s covered by its `RootRef` (the map-page precedent); a change rewrites one data page and the index. One new page type instead of three, no apply/fold code, but a page-split policy in the engine and uniqueness checks across pages at boot.
- **Option C (reuse the map directory)**: add `table_entries` to `CpLogDirHdr` (from its 8 `rsvd` bytes) and a third run of `RootRef`s. Least code, but a 2.3 `CpLogDir::decode` *refuses* such a region (`all.len() != total`), which is not "ignoring reserved bytes", and it ties table folds to map folds (`PreparedCp.dir` is owned by `otap-map`). I would not take it without an explicit ruling.

### 8.2 Decisions I could not derive from the code

1. Deltas for **both** tables, or only the snapshot table (the catalogue is already carried forward unchanged and changes only on operator action)? The brief names both.
2. One combined head (any table's deltas in CP order) or one head per table? Do the bases stay in `sb.snap_table`/`sb.catalog`, or move into the head page (which would free those two roots but change their meaning)?
3. Is the head **rewritten every CP with identity** (`cp_seq == sb.cp_seq`, like `cp_log_head`) or **carried forward** when nothing changed (like the catalogue)? The first costs one page per CP; the second has the shared-region damage problem (pitfall 12).
4. **Damage class** of a delta region or head at boot: tear the candidate (as `snap_table` does today), or drop-and-report (as the catalogue does)? A dropped snapshot delta can silently resurrect a deleted snapshot or lose a pin; a torn one takes the pool down for one damaged page that every candidate shares. A third option is "refuse the pool with a named region", leaving repair to `pool verify`.
5. **Where the table fold policy lives** (engine `FoldPolicy`, as E22 did for the map) and whether a table fold must coincide with a map fold (snapshots are created only at fold CPs, F13; deletions, rollbacks and resizes fold too, docs/08:819-820; pins and relocation rewrites do not).
6. Does the **pure apply/fold** function belong in `otap-format` (it is packing/validation with no policy, like `CpLogRec::map_entry`) or in the engine?
7. **Raise the engine floor** to "reads 2.4 and later" (`boot.rs:269`), as every minor so far did, since a 2.3 engine would otherwise boot a 2.4 pool on stale bases and ignore the deltas (nothing is released, so this is only a consistency question)?
8. While bumping: fix `parse_cp_stripes`'s `rsvd` strictness (pitfall 10) and the stale doc lines (section 7) in the same change, or leave them?
9. For attestation (`pool verify`): should the format crate gain a **partial identity check** for stripes whose writing CP is unknown (compare `agg_id`, `lseg`, `stripe_idx` only, and `max(desc.birth_cp) <= hdr.cp_seq`), or should the engine assemble that from `StripeSummary::parse` + `hdr()` itself?
10. Should `SnapRecord.deadlist_pba` / `SnapRootPage.deadlist` (always 0 / `NONE` today) be left untouched by Step 6, as I assume?
